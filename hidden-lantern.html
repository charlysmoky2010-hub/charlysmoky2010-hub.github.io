Original sample

The mirror that remembered first

The family covered every mirror before the funeral—except the small one in the attic. Three nights later, Elian found a handprint beneath its glass. When he wiped it away, the reflection did not copy him. It raised one finger to its lips, then pointed behind him. Elian turned. The room was empty. In the mirror, however, his father stood at the attic door—watching something crawl across the ceiling toward his son.
