Fictional customer brief

  • Candidate: Alex Morgan
  • Target: Operations Assistant
  • Target country: United Kingdom
  • Output language: English
  • Verified experience: warehouse order preparation, stock checks, shift handovers, basic Excel

CV experience section

Before

Warehouse Worker

Worked in warehouse. Responsible for orders. Checked products. Good team worker. Used computer sometimes.

Problems: generic wording, weak structure, unclear responsibility and no connection to the target role.

After

Warehouse Operations Assistant

  • Prepared and verified outbound orders against daily picking lists.
  • Reported stock discrepancies to the shift lead and supported corrective checks.
  • Maintained clear handover notes for incoming shifts.
  • Used basic Excel sheets to record order and stock information.

Improvement: clearer action language and role relevance without inventing numbers, responsibilities or qualifications.

Cover-letter opening

Before

I would welcome the opportunity to bring this practical experience into a more structured operations role.
