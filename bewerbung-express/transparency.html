Customer trust and operational clarity

Service transparency

This summary explains who provides the service, how AI-supported tools may be used, what the customer receives, who is responsible for verification and what the service does not do.

Core rule: AI-assisted, human-reviewed, fact-based and customer-controlled. No application is sent without the customer’s decision.

1. Service provider

The service is offered and personally supervised by Yusuf Şen, based in Emsdetten, Germany.

Contact: charlysmoky2010@gmail.com

2. What is included

  • One CV or résumé adapted to the agreed target role, country and language
  • One role-specific cover letter
  • One ready-to-send application email
  • PDF and editable delivery files
  • One minor correction round based on the original customer brief

Major changes of target role, target country or source information may require a new order or a separately agreed scope.

3. AI use and human responsibility

AI-supported tools may be used to structure customer information, compare the supplied job description with the customer’s verified experience and prepare initial wording.

A human reviews the final documents before delivery. AI output is not treated as automatically correct. The customer remains responsible for confirming that names, dates, employers, qualifications, achievements and contact details are accurate.

4. Facts we will not invent

We do not add employment, education, certificates, language levels, achievements, responsibilities, software skills, dates or personal information that the customer has not supplied or confirmed.

If information is incomplete or contradictory, we may pause delivery and request clarification.

5. Delivery timing

The stated 48-hour delivery window begins only after:

  • payment has been completed,
  • the target role or job advertisement has been supplied,
  • the customer’s work and education information is complete enough to proceed, and
  • any necessary clarification has been received.

Delays caused by missing or contradictory customer information do not count inside the production window.

6. Customer control and submission

The service does not automatically send applications to employers, recruiters, universities, authorities or other third parties.

The customer receives the final files, reviews them and decides where and when to submit them. Any later submission assistance must be separately and explicitly agreed.

7. Data handling

  • Customer documents are used only to deliver the purchased service.
  • Customer documents are not sold or reused as another customer’s application.
  • Documents are not sent to employers without the customer’s instruction.
  • The customer may request deletion of working files by email.
  • Payment and transaction records may be retained where required for accounting, fraud prevention or other legal obligations.

Customers should not send passwords, banking credentials or unrelated sensitive information.

8. Revisions

One minor revision round is included. It covers factual corrections, wording adjustments and small formatting changes that remain within the original target role and agreed scope.

A new target role, a substantially different application strategy or a complete rewrite based on newly supplied information may require a new scope.

9. Cancellation and refunds

Customers should contact us as soon as possible if they want to cancel. A cancellation requested before personalized work begins can be reviewed differently from a request made after drafting and review work has started.

Any refund decision will consider the work already completed and the customer’s applicable statutory rights. This operational summary does not limit mandatory consumer rights.

10. No outcome guarantee

The service improves document structure, clarity, language and alignment with the agreed role. It does not guarantee an interview, visa, admission, employment contract, salary level, recruiter response or any other result.

11. Complaints or corrections

Questions, deletion requests, correction requests or complaints can be sent to charlysmoky2010@gmail.com. Include the email address used for payment and do not send sensitive data that is unrelated to the request.
