What the check does — and does not

Supports comma-, semicolon- and tab-separated data, including quoted fields and line breaks. Exact repeated rows and missing cells are counted. This does not validate business transactions, merge separate files, detect fuzzy matches, check formulas or automatically fix CSVs. Direct XLSX upload is not supported; export as CSV first.
