Engineering pattern

How a capability moves toward use

1. Discover a candidate capability, strategy, lead or paid project from an allowed source.
2. Separate source provenance from truth; record missing evidence explicitly.
3. Run bounded qualification or fit checks. Unproven hard requirements remain blockers.
4. Route only accepted internal capabilities or owner-approved commercial actions.
5. Record observed outcomes and retire/revise approaches that underperform.
