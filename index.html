01 / ARTIST

Between image, water and time.

Yusuf Şen works across ebru, painting and moving image. The practice treats cultural memory, migration and material transformation as active forces rather than fixed subjects.

02 / EBRU

What is ebru?

Ebru is the art of shaping floating pigments on prepared water and transferring the image to paper. Here, water is not a neutral surface: it behaves, resists, carries traces and makes each image unrepeatable.

The image is not placed on water. It negotiates with water.
