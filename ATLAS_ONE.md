# ATLAS ONE

**Status:** Limited commercial pilot launched 2026-08-04.

## Single objective

Reach and successfully deliver the first verified paid customer order without expanding into another general-purpose AI system.

## Customer

Artists, small makers, cultural projects, and local businesses whose work is real but whose sellable offer is unclear.

## Commercial gates

ATLAS ONE must pass these gates in order:

1. A real visitor understands the offer without explanation.
2. A qualified prospect begins a conversation or checkout.
3. One live payment is verified in Stripe.
4. The agreed package is delivered within scope.
5. Customer acceptance, revision need, refund status, and actual margin are recorded.

## Rules

- No new platform, autonomous-agent framework, marketplace, subscription, or software layer before the first paid delivery is completed.
- A payment link is not revenue. Only a completed live payment is revenue evidence.
- Sales, followers, visibility, or income are never guaranteed.
- Automatic tax collection remains disabled until tax registrations and obligations are confirmed.
- External spending, outreach, account changes, and publication require explicit approval.
