# Anatolian Atlas — living Anatolian craft discovery

**Anatolian Atlas** is a multilingual cultural discovery and prospective maker–buyer network for traditional crafts originating in Türkiye. We document the difference between cultural interest, verified origin, approved makers, and actual availability. The project does not hold product inventory.

### Craft fields

- **Ebru marbling:** pigment on prepared water, original image-making, and artist provenance
- **İznik ceramics:** research into tile-making techniques, workshops, materials and origins
- **Telkari filigree:** evidence-based interest in metalwork, workmanship and material authenticity
- **Anatolian textiles:** textiles and production practices with clear care, fulfillment and attribution requirements

### Current public status — 15 October 2026

This is an early-stage research and enquiry initiative. No Atlas craft producer is currently verified and publicly approved for sale, and no Atlas products are presented here as available for immediate checkout. Submitting interest does not create a purchase, vendor approval, or contract. Product listings and payment will require separate checks.
