Script 1 · Psychological horror

The voicemail from tomorrow

At 2:13 every morning, Mara received a voicemail from her own number. The first message was only breathing. The second whispered her address. On the third night, the voice said, “Do not open the bedroom door.” Mara froze. Someone knocked from the hallway. Her phone lit up again. This time the voicemail was already playing: “You opened it.”

Production cue: begin with a phone screen close-up; end on the locked door moving inward.
Script 2 · Folklore horror

The guest who left no footprints

In the mountain village, every winter guest had to leave their shoes outside. One snowy night, an old woman arrived barefoot and asked for shelter. The family let her sleep beside the fire. By morning she was gone. Outside, the snow was untouched—except for a trail of footprints leading from the house toward the cemetery. None led in.

Production cue: use firelight, wind ambience and a final overhead shot of the one-way trail.
Script 3 · Uncanny mystery

The apartment above the top floor

Jonas lived on the building’s tenth and highest floor. Every night he heard furniture dragging above him. The landlord insisted there was no eleventh floor. Jonas finally climbed the emergency stairs and found one more landing. Its door carried his apartment number. Inside, someone was moving his furniture into the exact positions it would occupy the next morning.

Production cue: repeat the same room angle before and after the reveal to create visual recognition.
