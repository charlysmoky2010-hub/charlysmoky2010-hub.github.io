Seven flowers, five languages

Ebru is the art of forming floating pigment patterns on prepared water and transferring the design to paper. Floral compositions are associated with Necmeddin Okyay and later practitioners. Traditional technique names, such as Hatip and Taraklı, are not ordinary flower names.

The Turkish Ministry of Culture's Traditional Turkish Arts overview specifically mentions tulip, carnation, violet, poppy, hyacinth and rose in floral Ebru and describes daisy as a later addition by Mustafa Düzgünman. The UNESCO heritage description documents flowers and foliage among Ebru designs. See the source links below.

Türkçe

Ebru sanatı; boyaların yoğunlaştırılmış su üzerinde şekillendirilip kâğıda aktarılmasıyla uygulanır. Lale, karanfil, sümbül, menekşe, gül ve gelincik, çiçekli Ebru geleneğiyle ilişkilidir. Papatya motifi de bu repertuvara sonradan katılmıştır. Buradaki dijital çalışma kitabı geleneksel Ebru eğitimi değildir.

Deutsch

Die türkische Ebru-Kunst gestaltet Pigmente auf einer vorbereiteten Wasseroberfläche und überträgt sie auf Papier. Tulpen, Nelken, Hyazinthen, Veilchen, Rosen und Mohnblumen gehören zu den bekannten Blütenmotiven. Das verlinkte digitale Workbook ist eine kreative Zeichenübung, kein Kurs für traditionelles Ebru.

English

Turkish Ebru creates designs with pigments floating on a prepared liquid surface before transfer to paper. Tulips, carnations, hyacinths, violets, roses and poppies appear in the floral tradition. The digital workbook linked below explores drawing and abstract pattern-making; it does not teach authentic water-marbling technique.

Ελληνικά

Η παραδοσιακή τουρκική τέχνη Ebru δημιουργεί σχέδια με χρώματα στην επιφάνεια ειδικά προετοιμασμένου νερού και τα μεταφέρει σε χαρτί. Η τουλίπα, το γαρύφαλλο, ο υάκινθος, η βιολέτα και το τριαντάφυλλο είναι παραδείγματα ανθικών μοτίβων. Το ψηφιακό τετράδιο που ακολουθεί είναι άσκηση δημιουργικού σχεδίου, όχι μάθημα παραδοσιακού Ebru.

Español

El Ebru turco crea motivos con pigmentos sobre una superficie líquida preparada y después los transfiere al papel. Entre sus flores tradicionales figuran el tulipán, el clavel, el jacinto, la violeta, la rosa y la amapola. El cuaderno digital enlazado es una actividad de dibujo: no enseña la técnica artesanal tradicional.

Motif ≠ technique: Battal, Gelgit, Taraklı, Şal and Hatip are names of Ebru pattern families or techniques; their original Turkish names are preserved in serious catalogues. A new digitally drawn flower design should not automatically be presented as a handmade heritage Ebru artwork.
Three documented marbling traditions · One cultural atlas

Ebru, Suminagashi & Spanish marbled paper

Patterns transferred from a liquid surface to paper connect several decorative traditions. Their materials, histories, craftsmanship and uses are not interchangeable.

Tradition / original nameHow the pattern is madeHistorical context / use
Türkiye · EbruPrepared pigments float on a thickened liquid surface. The artist can guide colors with needles, combs and other tools; floral compositions are part of the repertoire.Marbled paper, calligraphic supports and decorative works; a distinct living craft tradition acknowledged by UNESCO.
Japan · 墨流し · SuminagashiInk is floated on water, often alternating with a spreading solution; touching brushes create rings that become waves and irregular circles. The design transfers to absorbent paper.Japanese paper decoration with surviving examples from the twelfth century; the Metropolitan Museum of Art documents its history and technique.
Spain · Papel jaspeado / papel de aguas / papel al bañoColors or pigments float on a liquid bath and the resulting marbled pattern is transferred to paper. Surviving Spanish bookbindings include drop-like and combed patterns.Part of the wider European paper-marbling tradition, especially associated with book endpapers and covers. The name is not a separate claim that all such marbling originated in Spain.

🇯🇵 Japon Suminagashi

墨流し — “suda akan mürekkep”. Suya bırakılan mürekkep, fırça ve yüzey gerilimi yardımıyla halkalar ve akışkan desenler oluşturur. Geleneksel Türk Ebrusu ile malzemesi ve uygulama biçimi aynı değildir. Japon kültürel geleneği olarak kendi adı korunur.

🇪🇸 İspanyol kâğıt ebrulama

Papel jaspeado (mermer desenli kâğıt), papel de aguas (su desenli kâğıt) ve papel al baño, İspanyol ciltçilik ve süsleme kaynaklarında kullanılan adlardır. Bunlar İspanya’daki Avrupa kâğıt ebrulama uygulamalarıdır; otomatik olarak “İspanya’da doğmuş ayrı bir Ebru tekniği” anlamına gelmez.

English · International terminology

Turkish Ebru is not a synonym for every marbling practice. Japanese Suminagashi is ink-floating marbling. In Spain, papel jaspeado and papel de aguas refer to historical marbled-paper practices, strongly linked to bookbinding.

Español · Tres tradiciones

El Ebru turco, el Suminagashi japonés y el papel jaspeado español comparten la transferencia de dibujos desde un líquido al papel, pero difieren en materiales, técnicas y tradiciones. En España, el papel marmoleado se usaba especialmente como guarda en encuadernaciones.

Catalog integrity: A Japanese-style digital concentric-ring image is not automatically an authentic handmade Suminagashi piece. Likewise a digital marbled-paper pattern is not a documented Spanish historical paper. Anatolian Atlas distinguishes heritage research, contemporary original digital artwork and physical handmade objects.
