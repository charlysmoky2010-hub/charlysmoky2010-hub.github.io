FINANCIAL ACCOUNTABILITY

Our financial boundary

Every allocation must add up to 100%. A draft policy cannot be portrayed as an agreed commission, and no amount enters the cooperative revenue ledger without an external payment reference and supporting evidence.

Legal and financial boundary

This software does not create a German eingetragene Genossenschaft (eG). Formal cooperative status requires founders, articles, required governance, audit association and registration. No funds are collected and no distribution is activated by this page.
