02 / MATERIAL OPEN QUESTIONS

What still needs proving

  1. Business identity. Check the merchant's legal identity, seller-of-record role and tax particulars independently of the website imprint.
  2. Manufacture and provenance. Request the producer's direct confirmation and traceable manufacturing evidence. Do not infer İznik provenance from a visual motif.
  3. Safety and intended use. Because the product is presented for food use, verify relevant food-contact material information and glaze compliance before recommending that use.
  4. Availability, shipping and returns. Confirm live stock, item variation, sale contract, total landed price and legally applicable return rights with the merchant.
  5. Images and data rights. Obtain written permission before image reuse, affiliate tracking, sharing shipment references or marketing a partnership.
