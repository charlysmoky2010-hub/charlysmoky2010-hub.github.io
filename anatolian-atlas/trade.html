02 / LIABILITY DESIGN

Separation is
not immunity.

01 / SELLER

Merchant of record

The independent merchant remains named on the sales contract and invoice. They organize packing, carriage, legally required cancellation, refunds and product remedies.

02 / ATLAS

Atlas as intermediary

Atlas records evidence, discloses affiliations, checks relevant marketplace obligations and routes complaints. It cannot contract out of its own negligence or statutory duties.
