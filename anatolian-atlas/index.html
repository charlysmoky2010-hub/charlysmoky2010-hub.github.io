04 / FAQ

Questions before you enquire

Can I purchase a specific craft item here?

Not yet. Prices and checkout are not offered here until the seller, product details and delivery obligations have been checked.

Does Anatolian Atlas make or store inventory?

No. The concept is a digital discovery and trade network; production and fulfillment remain with the actual maker, if a future agreement is verified.

Does an application mean that I am an approved seller?

No. Maker profiles and products require separate review before any public listing.

What information is useful in a sourcing enquiry?

Name the type of craft, approximate quantity, destination, use case and any provenance or quality requirements. The form explains its own personal-data collection.
