    04 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

    What is real here?

    Can I buy these 3D objects?

    No. They are digitally constructed material studies, not scans of stocked products. Anatolian Atlas has not confirmed purchasable maker listings.

    Is the guide generative AI?

    No. It is a local, transparent, rules-based sourcing assistant. It does not fabricate merchants or send your selections to a server.

    Who produces and ships future craft orders?

    Any eventual purchase would be handled by a separately verified independent maker or merchant, subject to clear written terms. Anatolian Atlas does not warehouse products.

    Does the site earn commissions?

    Not currently. There are no verified affiliate partnerships, paid maker listings or commission-backed product recommendations. Any future sponsored content will be clearly identified after approval.

    What happens when I enquire?

    The existing enquiry service opens separately. Submitting its form can send your details for review. Viewing this page and using the guide alone sends no personal sourcing selections.
