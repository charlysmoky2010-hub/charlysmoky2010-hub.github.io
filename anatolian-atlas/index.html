    02 / Independent buyer's guide

    Buying İznik ceramics or Telkari? Four checks before you order

    These questions help you evaluate a maker or retailer wherever you buy. They are research guidance, not a certification or recommendation of any unverified seller.

    01 · Who made it?

    Request the workshop's name, production location and whether the object is handmade, studio-produced or mass-manufactured. Traditional inspiration alone does not establish where an item was made.

    02 · What is it made of?

    For İznik-style ceramics, ask about the ceramic body, glaze, dimensions and whether food-contact suitability is documented if you intend to use it with food. For Telkari, ask for the actual metal composition and any relevant hallmark or material evidence.

    03 · Who takes responsibility?

    Confirm the identity of the actual seller, the final price, return conditions and the party responsible for shipment, breakage or non-delivery. For deliveries into the EU, ask about taxes, import formalities and relevant product compliance.

    04 · Can the claim be checked?

    Look for photographs of the real object, workshop attribution, size and care information. An AI-generated description, an attractive stock image or an unverified origin claim is not proof of provenance.

    05 / FAQ

    Questions before you enquire

    Can I purchase a specific craft item here?

    Not yet. Prices and checkout are not offered here until the seller, product details and delivery obligations have been checked.

    Does Anatolian Atlas make or store inventory?

    No. The concept is a digital discovery and trade network; production and fulfillment remain with the actual maker, if a future agreement is verified.

    Does an application mean that I am an approved seller?

    No. Maker profiles and products require separate review before any public listing.

    What information is useful in a sourcing enquiry?

    Name the type of craft, approximate quantity, destination, use case and any provenance or quality requirements. The form explains its own personal-data collection.

    Does Anatolian Atlas earn from recommended products?

    There are currently no paid affiliate links, paid maker listings or verified commission partnerships on this guide. If commercial referrals are introduced after programme and legal approval, they will be clearly labelled as advertising. The actual merchant would remain responsible for pricing, sale and fulfillment.
