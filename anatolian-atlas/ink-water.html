PRACTICE 01

Follow the current

Train your hand to describe movement without outlining an object.

THE PROMPT

Choose one starting point. Draw three slow, uninterrupted curves travelling toward an edge. Add smaller tributaries that gently follow the largest curve.

A SMALL CHALLENGE

Make one line thick, one line fine, and leave one line unbroken. Let the white gaps carry part of the rhythm.

REFLECT

Where does your eye enter the composition? Where does it leave?

What is inside

A creative starting point — not another blank page.

Designed for adults who want to try abstract art journaling or colour-and-pattern practice at home. Choose a palette, sketch the direction of flowing lines, explore positive and negative space, and review your work — without needing special marbling equipment. Edition 02 includes six AI-assisted digital prints and twelve guided drawing and composition exercises inspired by Turkish Ebru, Japanese Suminagashi and Spanish papel de aguas, with printing guidance and a personal-use licence. It is a digital download, not a shipped or handmade object.

Who this printable is for

Adult beginners exploring art journals, colour studies and abstract pattern-making. Use pencils, markers or a drawing tablet. This workbook introduces drawing and composition inspired by Ebru, Suminagashi and papel de aguas; it is not a hands-on traditional paper-marbling technique course.
