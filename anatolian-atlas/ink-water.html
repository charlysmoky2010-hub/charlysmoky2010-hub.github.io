What is inside

A creative starting point — not another blank page.

Designed for adults who want to try abstract art journaling or colour-and-pattern practice at home. Choose a palette, sketch the direction of flowing lines, explore positive and negative space, and review your work — without needing special marbling equipment. The kit includes four digitally drawn art prints, eight self-guided creative exercises, printing guidance and a personal-use licence. It is a digital download, not a shipped or handmade object.

18 + 18pages in two PDF editions: A4 (210 × 297 mm) and US Letter (8.5 × 11 in).
04original digital art prints in each page-size edition.

Artwork disclosure. These are original AI-assisted digitally drawn designs inspired by marbling aesthetics. They are not photographs, scans or reproductions of handmade Turkish Ebru paintings. Print results vary by printer and paper. The files are for personal home use, with limited non-commercial classroom use for worksheets; commercial reproduction and resale are not included.

Who this printable is for

Adult beginners exploring art journals, colour studies and abstract pattern-making. Use pencils, markers or a drawing tablet. This workbook introduces marbling-inspired design practice; it is not a hands-on traditional Turkish Ebru technique course.
