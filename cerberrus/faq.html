Frequently asked questions

What is a Public Signal Brief?

A concise, bounded public-source situational research brief for a selected area. It summarizes observations and explicitly states source health and limits.

What sources are used?

Current layers can include OpenSky, USGS, Launch Library 2, CelesTrak and available public-camera registries. Coverage and freshness vary by source.

Does CERBERRUS track private people?

No. Private-person tracking is outside the product boundary.

Is this emergency or law-enforcement intelligence?

No. It is situational research, not emergency, safety, law-enforcement, medical, legal, financial or investment advice.

Will I receive marketing follow-ups after a free sample?

No unsolicited follow-up. One request means one sample unless you initiate further contact.

What are the prices?

€19 for a pilot brief, €49 for one custom city/region brief, and €149 for an organization pilot covering up to three areas plus a comparison summary.
