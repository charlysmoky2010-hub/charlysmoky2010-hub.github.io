OSINT area brief vs continuous monitoring

The two products solve different problems. Continuous monitoring emphasizes ongoing alerts and operational feeds. A bounded area brief emphasizes a readable snapshot, explainability and controlled scope.

Area briefContinuous monitoring
Time modelSnapshotOngoing
Reader effortLowHigher
OutputReadable reportAlerts/dashboard
ScopeExplicitly boundedPotentially broad

Why CERBERRUS starts with briefs

A brief is easier to audit, easier to explain and less likely to imply capabilities the data cannot support. It also gives buyers a concrete artifact rather than another interface to learn.

Privacy boundary

CERBERRUS does not offer private-person tracking. Its product positioning is public/open-source situational research.
