Why a brief instead of a dashboard?

A dashboard asks the reader to interpret streams. A CERBERRUS brief compresses a bounded snapshot into readable observations, source health, context, provenance and limitations. It is situational research — not emergency, law-enforcement, medical, legal, financial or investment advice.
